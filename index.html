Good to know

Curious about DFC?

A quick introduction to how students can get involved and make the most of the club.

Who can be part of Defence Focus Club?

Students of TKMCE who are interested in defence awareness, leadership, service and related learning activities can connect with the club.

Do I need prior defence knowledge?

No. The club is a place to start learning, explore reliable information and develop your interests step by step.

What happens during club activities?

Activities may include awareness sessions, expert interactions, workshops, discussions and collaborative campus initiatives. Follow official club announcements for confirmed events.

Where can I find reliable career information?

Use the official recruitment and service websites linked through Defence Track AI. Always verify eligibility, dates and instructions on the relevant official website.

Learn. Prepare. Contribute.
